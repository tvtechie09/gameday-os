## Sign in

Use your individually provisioned pilot account. Do not share credentials. Venue operators must not use a Platform Admin account. If sign-in fails, stop retrying after two attempts and contact the internal GameDay pilot owner through the agreed launch channel.

Never include a password, access link, token, or private customer data in feedback or screenshots.

## What to report

Use **More → Send Feedback** for product feedback such as confusing navigation, unclear labels, display problems, or a suggestion about the GameDay experience.

Use **Work Orders** for an operational venue problem that needs ownership and resolution, such as a broken scoreboard, damaged gate, or field-maintenance issue.

For an immediate safety incident, follow the venue's emergency and radio/PA procedures first. Pilot feedback is not an emergency channel.

## Access failure

Report the role, screen, time, and non-sensitive error wording to the internal GameDay pilot owner. Do not send passwords, tokens, or full browser-storage captures.
