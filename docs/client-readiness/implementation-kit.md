# Founding-Venue Implementation Kit

## Information requested from the customer

- Legal organization and venue names, address, timezone, and primary operating contact.
- Field names, parent/split-field relationships, sports, and any public wayfinding labels.
- Schedule source, export format, sample file/feed, season dates, and the person authorized to resolve mapping conflicts.
- Primary operator, backup operator, escalation contact, and staff invitation list.
- Weather decision policy, emergency contacts, PA/radio fallback, and venue closure authority.
- Existing scoreboard, audio, camera, signage, and network inventory. Inventory does not imply an integration is supported.
- Logo, colors, sponsor roster, prohibited sponsor categories, and approved public links.

## GameDay setup sequence

1. Provision the organization, venue, fields, and known hardware inventory.
2. Import a sample schedule and resolve every field mapping.
3. Configure weather monitoring and operating contacts.
4. Invite staff with the minimum required permissions.
5. Generate and print field QR codes.
6. Load the full pilot schedule and spot-check source identity, time, teams, and field.
7. Run the six-step rehearsal in Pilot Launch.
8. Resolve serious incidents and record go/no-go approval.
9. Operate one real date and review the evidence within 24 hours.

## Definition of successful onboarding

- Venue staff can run the day without a developer.
- A schedule change is made once and appears correctly on the operator and public experiences.
- A parent can scan a QR code on cellular data and understand where to go and what changed.
- The primary operator, backup, and escalation contact are named and reachable.
- Every intervention is recorded, assigned, and reviewed.
