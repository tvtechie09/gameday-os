# Founding-Venue Buyer Demo

Use this 15-minute story with a venue director or general manager. It is not a product tour.

## Before the meeting

1. Open **Admin → Demo Readiness**.
2. Select a disposable demo tenant and click **Prepare reference demo**.
3. Confirm every required check is ready.
4. Open Command Center, a public field QR page, Sponsor Campaigns, End of Day, and Pilot Launch in separate tabs.
5. Test the public page from a phone using cellular data.

## The story

### 0:00 — Start with their Saturday

Ask: “When one field falls 30 minutes behind, who notices first, and how does everybody else find out?” Listen before opening the product.

### 2:00 — Understand the day

Open Command Center. Show the live games, fields needing attention, schedule pulse, and the most urgent item. The claim is simple: GameDay turns the schedule into an operating view for the venue.

### 5:00 — Communicate one confirmed change

Show the delay and all-clear workflow. Explain that staff publish one confirmed change and the venue/public experience reads from the same operating record. Do not claim every external league system updates automatically.

### 8:00 — Show the family experience

Scan a printed field QR over cellular data. Show field identity, current or next game, active alert, schedule, directions, and notification controls. No app download should be required.

### 10:00 — Show accountable operations

Show one assigned work order and the end-of-day carryover. The value is not another dashboard; it is knowing what needs attention, who owns it, and what remains tomorrow.

### 12:00 — Show optional sponsor proof

If the demo has a campaign, show verified versus modeled delivery and any make-good. Do not present demo counts as customer revenue.

### 14:00 — Make the pilot ask

Offer one venue, one operating date, imported real schedule, staff rehearsal, QR launch, and a go/no-go checkpoint. Ask: “What would you need to see to trust this for one real Saturday?” Their answer becomes the only candidate backlog.

## Truthful answers

- **Do you replace GameChanger, SportsEngine, TeamSnap, or registration?** No. GameDay is the venue operating layer and hands off to incumbent systems.
- **Do integrations update automatically?** CSV, iCal, and public-feed imports work. Direct providers are described only according to the integration maturity matrix.
- **Can you control our scoreboard?** Not generally. Daktronics has a read-only bridge; physical control requires a separately verified deployment.
- **Do you process family payments?** No. The venue pays by invoice or purchase order; families are not charged.
