# Founding-Venue Pilot Proposal

## Objective

Prove that GameDay can help one multi-field venue import its day, operate it, communicate changes, and produce an accountable closeout without replacing the venue's existing registration or scoring systems.

## Scope

- One venue and one agreed operating date.
- Venue and field configuration.
- One real schedule import and mapping review.
- Staff roles for a primary and backup operator.
- Public venue/field QR experiences.
- Weather, delay, field-status, and announcement rehearsal.
- End-of-day operating evidence.
- Optional sponsor proof when the venue supplies a real campaign.

## Responsibilities

GameDay configures the pilot, trains the primary and backup operators, supports rehearsal, and reviews incidents. The venue supplies accurate schedule/field information, assigns decision authority, tests on its real devices/network, and participates in the post-pilot review.

## Success criteria

- Required Pilot Launch checks pass before go-live.
- The venue operates without an unresolved high/urgent incident.
- Parents can use printed QR codes without an account.
- Confirmed changes appear correctly in operator and public views.
- Developer-required interventions are recorded and trend toward zero.
- The venue agrees to continue, declines with a documented reason, or requests a clearly scoped second test.

## Commercial posture

Use the current founding-venue pricing sheet as an anchor, not an unchangeable quote. Bill by invoice or purchase order. GameDay does not charge families or process registration payments. Hardware and unsupported integrations require separate scope and validation.
