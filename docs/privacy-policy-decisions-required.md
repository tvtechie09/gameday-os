# Privacy policy decisions required

Legal/business approval is required before GameDay enables deactivation or erasure execution:

1. Identity, operational, Work Order/photo, communications, analytics, feedback, provider, and security-audit retention durations.
2. How a requester proves account ownership and how a guardian proves authority for a child; age/region-specific handling.
3. Which administrators may approve requests, whether two-person approval is required, and support impersonation restrictions.
4. Which audit actor references must remain, may be pseudonymized, or must be deleted under applicable law.
5. Whether and how source-identity tombstones suppress provider resync, for how long, and when a fresh canonical person is permitted.
6. Treatment of shared guardian identifiers, siblings, multi-household guardians, team rosters, and other users' relationship records.
7. Whether operational authorship (announcements, Work Orders, schedules) is retained under a pseudonymous actor.
8. Export response deadline, secure delivery method, request audit, download expiry, and requester notification.
9. Whether optional profile notes, support feedback, photos containing people, and provider asserted values are exportable or redacted.
10. Customer contract responsibilities when an organization is data controller and GameDay is processor.

Until these decisions are approved, the implementation exposes export and impact preview only. There is no destructive endpoint, scheduled erasure, single-click action, or implied self-service right.
