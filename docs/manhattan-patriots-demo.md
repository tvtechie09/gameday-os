# Manhattan Patriots GameDay OS concept demo

This private, synthetic demo shows how GameDay OS could coordinate a Manhattan Patriots football Saturday across game-day leadership, coaches, families and venue stakeholders. It is a concept preview, not evidence of a partnership or live integration.

Start at `/demo/manhattan-patriots/gameday`.

## Seven-minute walkthrough

1. **Command / Arrival (90 seconds)** — Show the four River Valley tackle divisions, the synthetic volunteer coverage issue and the connected-systems boundary. Acknowledge the issue to demonstrate the browser-only interaction.
2. **Coach (60 seconds)** — Open the coach view. It distills one synthetic JV game into kickoff, warm-up and checklist context without exposing a roster or child data.
3. **Family (60 seconds)** — Open the family view. Lincoln-Way West and its address are verified; parking, entrance, seating and amenity guidance are deliberately labeled hypothetical.
4. **Venue (60 seconds)** — Explain that the field graphic is a conceptual operations map, not a Lincoln-Way West layout. There is no scoreboard, clock, PA, signage or video control.
5. **Weather delay (90 seconds)** — Select **2. Weather delay**. Two later games move 45 minutes, drafts change by audience, and the game-day issue becomes visible. Nothing is sent and no safety decision is automated.
6. **Recovery (60 seconds)** — Select **3. Recovery**. The revised schedule persists after a simulated human all-clear, and the synthetic activity trail shows where each decision came from.

## What is real

- The Manhattan Patriots offer tackle football, flag football and cheerleading.
- Tackle home games are at Lincoln-Way West High School.
- Tackle practices are at Manhattan Intermediate School.
- Tackle uses Super Lightweight, Lightweight, Junior Varsity and Varsity divisions.
- Lincoln-Way West's verified address is 21701 S. Gougar Road, New Lenox, IL 60451.
- The organization website says cheer communications use email and/or TeamSnap. This does not establish a GameDay integration or use by every Patriots program.

Sources checked November 18, 2026: https://manhattanpatriots.com/, https://manhattanpatriots.com/tackle/, https://manhattanpatriots.com/cheer/, and https://www.lw210.org/o/west/page/welcome/.

## What is simulated

The demo date, opponents, people, roster status, times, game changes, volunteer assignments, issues and activity history; schedule imports, scoreboard/game-clock adapters, PA/signage routing and video endpoints; all interactions, alerts and acknowledgments; and all parking, gates, seating, warm-up zones, concessions, amenities, equipment status and physical venue layout.

GameDay is presented as a coordination layer for existing services, not a replacement for registration, team communication, native scorekeeping, streaming or venue control. This demo does not process payments, register participants, publish messages, operate devices or make safety decisions.

No real children's or family data is used. Any future child-data workflow requires joint Legal, Product, Engineering and QA review before real data is introduced.
