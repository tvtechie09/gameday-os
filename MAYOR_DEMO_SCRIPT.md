# Crossroads Mayor Demo Script

GameDay OS positioning: **GameDay OS is the Connected Venue Operating System.**

Crossroads positioning: **Wintrust Crossroads is the flagship reference implementation for a connected municipal sports venue.**

## Opening Statement

Today we are showing Crossroads as more than a collection of fields. We are showing it as a connected venue where families, tournament directors, venue staff, leadership, TVs, QR codes, scoreboards, maintenance workflows, and future Village communications can all work from the same operating model.

This demo uses realistic Crossroads data. Some items are live in the local demo, some are platform foundations, and vendor integrations are clearly labeled as future or partner-required.

## 10-Minute Demo Flow

1. **Crossroads Today** - 60 seconds  
   Open `/demo/crossroads/today`. Show the venue front door: event, games today, visitor estimate, live fields, delayed fields, announcements, and quick links.

2. **Family Experience** - 75 seconds  
   Open Family Experience. Show South Lot to Field 6B, current game, schedule, and Visitor Services.

3. **QR Context Demo** - 45 seconds  
   From Crossroads Today, show how one Field 6 scan can become parent, staff, or tournament context.

4. **Tournament Operations** - 75 seconds  
   Show delayed games, behind-schedule games, and readiness checks.

5. **Operations Center** - 90 seconds  
   Show Today, Maintenance, Assets, Staff, Safety, Communications, Community, Analytics, and Future Roadmap framing.

6. **TV Dashboard / Venue Channels** - 75 seconds  
   Open `/demo/crossroads/tv`. Show Crossroads Live, Tournament HQ, Village Events, Weather & Safety, Sponsor Rotation, and Menu/Concessions.

7. **Media Engine** - 60 seconds  
   Open `/demo/crossroads/media`. Show mock Field 6B camera, score overlay from Game State Engine, routing to bar TVs/family app/Tournament HQ, and future livestream destination. Talking point: “Create media once, distribute it everywhere.”

8. **Weather Delay Story** - 90 seconds  
   In Presentation Mode, toggle Weather Delay. Show public alert, tournament impact, and operations communication.

9. **Staff Mode** - 60 seconds  
   Open `/demo/crossroads/staff`. Show assigned requests, open incidents, tasks, asset issues, and quick actions.

10. **Executive Summary** - 75 seconds  
   Open `/demo/crossroads/gm`. Show weekend games hosted, estimated visitors, maintenance, assets, utilization, and revenue opportunities.

11. **Crossroads 2030** - 75 seconds  
    Close with Future Vision phases: Connected Venue Software, Read-Only Integrations, Approved Vendor Integrations, Smart Venue / AI / Wayfinding, and Connected Municipality.

## 15-Minute Extended Demo Flow

Use the 10-minute flow, then add:

- Crossroads map hotspots and field/play-surface hierarchy.
- Field 6B public page and QR-style entry.
- Maintenance request intake from a field, concession, restroom, or equipment endpoint.
- Asset Register detail and related maintenance.
- Community Dashboard: Village events, Explore New Lenox, sponsor/community partner highlights.
- Future integration boundaries: Daktronics read-only, GameChanger future source-of-truth, signage/POS/access control/emergency systems partner-required.

## Key Value Points

- Families find fields faster and need fewer staff interruptions.
- Tournament operations improve because schedule, delays, readiness, and field state are visible.
- Staff issues become visible through maintenance, asset, incident, and task views.
- Existing TVs, QR codes, scoreboards, and field pages become more valuable as connected communication surfaces.
- Future field cameras, score overlays, venue TV channels, family app previews, and tournament displays can share one media routing layer.
- Village communication improves through venue announcements, community event promotion, and tourism/visitor messaging placeholders.
- Crossroads becomes a flagship connected municipal venue and a repeatable reference model for other complexes.

## Closing Statement

GameDay OS does not ask Crossroads to replace every existing system on day one. It creates the connected venue layer above them: the shared operating model for families, tournaments, staff, leadership, displays, scoreboards, safety communications, sponsors, and future municipal integrations.

The result is a venue that is easier to navigate, easier to operate, easier to communicate through, and easier to grow strategically.

## Professional Demo Disclaimers

- Daktronics data in this demo is mock/read-only and does not control physical scoreboards.
- GameChanger as source-of-truth is a future integration path.
- Digital signage control requires approved signage/player integrations.
- POS/menu integrations require approved vendor access.
- Camera ingest, PTZ control, OBS/production systems, RTMP/livestream destinations, recording, and replay are future integrations.
- Access control is role-awareness/future integration only.
- Emergency system integrations require Village, venue, and vendor approval.
